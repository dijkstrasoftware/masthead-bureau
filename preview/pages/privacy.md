We keep this short because we collect very little.

## What we collect

When you email us, we keep your message and contact details for as long as we are in conversation, and for seven years afterwards if you become a client, as Dutch law requires.

This website sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics beyond anonymous server logs, which are deleted after 30 days.

## Who we share it with

Nobody, except the email and accounting providers we use to run the studio. They are all based in the European Union.

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, and ask us to delete it. Write to hello@fieldoffice.example.
