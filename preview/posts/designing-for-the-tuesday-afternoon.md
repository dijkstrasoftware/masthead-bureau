Brand guidelines are read by tired people with deadlines. Write them that way.

A brief usually describes a solution. Somebody has already decided they need a new logo, or a new website, or a campaign — and the brief is the instructions for making it.

## Start with the problem

Our first job is to find the problem the solution was meant to solve. Sometimes the brief survives intact. More often, the real work turns out to be somewhere nearby.

- Who asked for this, and why now?
- What happens if we do nothing?
- How will we know it worked?

> The most useful thing a designer can do is ask a better question.

None of this is new. But it is easy to forget when a deadline is close and the brief is already signed.
